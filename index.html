Our Story

Where Coffee Meets Capybaras

Nestled in the vibrant Son Tra district of Da Nang, CapyBara Cafe is Vietnam's first cafe dedicated to the world's largest — and friendliest — rodents. Our resident capybaras live in a spacious, naturalistic habitat and love meeting guests over a cup of carefully crafted coffee.

We source all our beans from the Central Highlands and partner with local farms for our pastries and fresh fruit drinks. Every sip supports sustainable agriculture in Vietnam.
